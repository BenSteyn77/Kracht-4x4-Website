Our Story

Started by 4x4 enthusiasts, still run by them.

Kracht began the way most good workshops do — upgrading our own vehicles for better performance, better fuel economy and more comfort on long trips, then doing the same for friends and family.

That grew into a fitment centre for anyone who wants their vehicle built properly, with a wide network of suppliers covering everything from performance and power to comfort and long-haul reliability.
