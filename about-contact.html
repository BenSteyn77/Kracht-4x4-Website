IT’S MORE THAN
A VEHICLE.

It’s the daily drive, the workhorse and the way to your next campsite. We understand that because we’re outdoor and 4x4 enthusiasts ourselves.

Kracht started with upgrades to our own vehicles, then those of friends and family. That passion grew into a fitment centre for people who want the same performance, comfort and capability, with a team to take care of the work.

From performance exhausts and suspension to interior protection and pre-trip maintenance, we bring practical care and attention to the details.
